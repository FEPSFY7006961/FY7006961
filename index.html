Abstract

Introduction

As computer technologies and social media continue to be omnipresent everywhere in our lives, the threat of cyber-attack has been under-recognized. Cyber-attacks are embedded everywhere behind the internet especially the social media. A cyberattack is a deliberate attempt to gain unauthorized access to a computer network, computer system or digital device. The goal is to steal, expose, alter, disable or destroy data, applications or other assets. (Jonker and Krantz, no date) Cyber-attacks comes in different form and types. Although it sounds scary, there are some ways to prevent the harm from cyber-attacks.

Analysis and Discussion

      There are various type of cyber-attacks. such as phishing, malware, DDoS attacks, password cracking, and security threats to social media accounts, including phishing attacks, information theft, and account takeovers. (İmanova and Mahmudova, 2025) For each type of cyber-attack, there is also a breakdown of different form of that cyber-attack. 
      The first type of cyber-attack is Malware. Some sub-form of Malware are worms, Torjan horses and spyware. Worms autonomously spreading or duplicating itself without human intervention (Palit, 2025) Torjan horses infiltrates a computer by posing as a genuine program (Palit, 2025) Spyware when users inadvertently check on unfamiliar links or open unknown attachments, leading to simultaneous download of spyware (Palit, 2025)
      Malware has a strong connection with the activity behind social media. Cybercriminals use social media to spread malware by embedding malicious links to posts, advertisements and messages which might lead to installation of ransomware, spyware or keyloggers on devices. (no author, 2025)
      The second type of cyber attack is Viruses. Sub-types of Viruses are File infector virus, Boot sector virus and Email virus (or known as phishing). File infector virus targets executable files and infect them within computer system. (Palit, 2025) Boot sector virus targets the critical boot sector of hard drive bypassing the standard security measures. (Palit, 2025) Email virus/ phishing lead to user opening hyperlinks attached with emails  (Palit, 2025)
      Out of all different forms of viruses, phishing is one of the most common cyber attacks and has a hgh risk of hazard on social media. Attackers stablish phony profiles, sites or messages that impersonate real people or organisations. Fraudulent messages frequently include malicious links which take users to phony websites designed to steal login credentials or private, personal information e.g. financial information. (no author, 2025)
      The third type of cyber attack is Fake profiles. Fake profiles are always a cyber attack that is known to most people but most people never take any action on it to protect their social media accounts. Fake profiles can be used to manipulate users into financial scams, blackmail or identity theft. (no author, 2025) There are different forms of performing fake profiles. The first form is Account takeovers where cyber criminals gain access to social media accounts through data breaches or weak passwords. After compromising, accounts are used to scam contacts, spread malware, or demand ransom for account recovery (no author, 2025)The second form is Crypto and investment scams where different fraudulent investment opportunities promising guaranteed profits are all over social media. Cybercriminals use fake endorsements from celebrities or influencers to lure victims into investing fake cryptocurrency schemes. (no author, 2025) The last form of performing fake profiles is Job scams where fake job offers on platform of social media convince job seekers into providing personal information for non existent employment. (no author, 2025)
      Knowing the different forms of cyber attack and the impact they can have on social media, it is also equally important to know and understand the ways to avoid or prevent different threats of cyber attack so that we can surf on social media at our safest. Firstly as an easy procedure from the user, using strong password is a primary action for users. It prevent attacks and reduces the risk of threats to our social media. Strong passwords should be regularly updated and in a form of combining special characters, upper and lower case letters and numbers. (Coursea Staff, 2026) Avoiding repeating the same password for different account and systems is also important because it increase the risk of giving attackers access to personal information. (Coursea Staff, 2026)
      Moreover, to avoid the harm from phishing, be mindful before clicking links and checking the emails address to ensure it appears legitimate. (Coursea Staff, 2026) Do not open emails from people unkown or click on unknown links attached in emails. (Etuh, Bakpo and Agozie, 2022) 
      A tactics that is useful for social media accounts is to implement browser security policies in devices e.g. block pop-ups. (Etuh, Bakpo and Agozie, 2022) A common security practise is to install a firewall. Firewalls help to prevent various attacks and threats, such as malware, hacking and unauthorized access to social media accounts. Firewall works by controlling the network traffic moving through system so it stops any suspicious activity it deems potentially harmful to the computer. (Coursea Staff, 2026) Another common security practise is to ensure up-to-date antivirus software on the device as a primary line of defence. (Etuh, Bakpo and Agozie, 2022) Updating software inside devices is also crucial because up-to-date software systems are more resilient than outdated versions which is prone to having weaknesses. Updates can correct any flaws in the software and keep our social media accounts as safe as possible. (Coursea Staff, 2026)
